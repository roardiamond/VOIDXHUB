Passwords are stored using strong one-way hashing. We do not store plain-text passwords. Access to administrative systems is restricted. We use industry-standard practices to protect data, but no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited data with payment processors or infrastructure providers only as necessary to operate the service, or when required by law.

We use local storage and similar technologies to keep you logged in and remember preferences. You can clear these through your browser settings.

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account data by contacting us through our official Telegram support channel. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page with an updated date.
